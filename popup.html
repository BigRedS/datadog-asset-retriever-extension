Datadog Exporter

Export the current monitor or dashboard list to CSV (Name, URL, Query).

Paste Datadog URLs below (one per line). The extension will open each page, read its name, and export a CSV (URL, Name). URLs without a scheme (e.g. app.datadoghq.com/…) are treated as https://.

If you open a Datadog monitor or dashboard list page and click the extension again, it will automatically show the list export for that page.
